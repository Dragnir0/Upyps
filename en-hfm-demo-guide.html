How to open and use an HFM demo account

Last updated: 28 October 2026

A demo account is a practice account with virtual funds: no real money is involved. Leverage is adjustable on live and demo accounts, and the platform is available on MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5, WebTrader and the HFM app.

Six steps to explore the demo

  1. Open the account. From HFM's website, create a free demo account. No deposit is needed for the demo.
  2. Choose the platform. WebTrader runs in the browser, with no installation. MT4 and MT5 are downloaded; the HFM app runs on mobile.
  3. Find your way around. Identify the instrument list, the chart, the order window and the tab that shows your positions and balance.
  4. Place a practice order. Pick an instrument, a very small lot size, then a stop loss level (the maximum loss you set) and a take profit level (the closing level). Watch how the balance moves.
  5. Read the history. After closing, open the history: entry and exit price, size, result. Understand every line.
  6. Repeat. Try several lot sizes and leverage levels to see their effect on the balance, over several days.

What a demo does not show

With real money, the psychological pressure changes. Execution conditions and costs can also differ from those of a demo. A demo shows how the platform works; it says nothing about what you would obtain with real money.

Checklist before considering a live account

  • I understand the risks of Forex and CFDs (see the full warning).
  • I know which HFM entity would provide the service and what that implies (see the HFM entity and regulation page).
  • I have checked that these services are permitted in my country of residence.
  • I know the deposit and withdrawal terms.
  • I would only risk what I can afford to lose.
