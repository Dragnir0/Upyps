Trader, investor or manager

According to HFM's official presentation, the platform also offers shared-management services. They carry risks: always check the terms on hfm.com.

Independent trader

You open an account and make your own decisions, using MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5, WebTrader or the HFM app.

Subscriber or investor

With Copy Trading, a subscriber copies a strategy provider's positions, keeps access to their MT4 or MT5 account, chooses the volume allocation and can withdraw funds at any time, according to HFM. With PAMM, funds are entrusted to a manager; HFM states a start from $250.

Strategy provider or manager

A strategy provider shares positions with subscribers: HFM states up to five strategy accounts, twice-monthly payments and performance fees of up to 50%. A PAMM manager manages investors' funds, with positions adjusted automatically.

Copying a trader or entrusting funds to a manager guarantees no result and may lead to losses. Past performance does not predict future results. Upyps does not manage any funds and does not recommend, rate or select any strategy provider or manager.

Frequently asked questions

No. Upyps is an independent HFM partner and is not HFM. Links lead to HF Markets (SV) Ltd, incorporated in St. Vincent & the Grenadines.

HFM offers a free demo account with virtual funds. The terms are shown on HFM's website.

Does Upyps give trading advice or signals?

No. Upyps does not publish signals, investment advice or market opinions. The content is general marketing communication.

HFM offers shared-management services (Copy Trading and PAMM). This guarantees no result and may lead to losses. Upyps does not manage any funds and does not recommend any manager. Check the terms and availability in your country on hfm.com.
