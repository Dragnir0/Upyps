HFM account guide: Cent, Zero, Pro, Premium

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This guide explains how to read the differences between HFM account types. The descriptions reflect what HFM publishes in its West Africa client presentation; conditions can vary by entity and country. Always check the current terms on hfm.com before opening an account.

AccountHFM's descriptionFeatures published by HFM
Cent“The account for beginner traders”Swap-free trading; cent lots (one cent lot = 0.01 of a standard lot, or 1,000 units); margin requirements from 10 US cents.
Zero“The account for all traders”Raw spreads with no mark-up; commissions from $3 per lot; swap-free.
Pro“The account for professional traders”Very low spreads; no commission; swap-free; high leverage.
Premium“The account for retail traders”Swap-free; no minimum deposit; no commission; access via MT4, MT5, WebTrader or the HFM app.

How to read these differences

  • Spread and commission: the spread is the gap between the buy and sell price; a commission is an extra charge, often per lot. An account can have a spread only, or a tighter spread plus a commission: compare the total cost, not a single figure.
  • Swap: a charge or credit applied when a position stays open overnight. “Swap-free” means this charge is not applied on the instruments concerned; terms and conditions apply.
  • Lot size: a standard lot is 100,000 units of the base currency. A cent lot is 1,000. The larger the lot, the more each price move weighs on the balance, in both directions.
  • Leverage: it lets you open a position larger than the deposited capital. It amplifies gains and losses alike. The maximum level depends on the entity, the country and the instrument.

Five questions to ask yourself before comparing

  1. 1. How much can I lose without it affecting my daily life?
  2. 2. Do I understand how a lot, a spread and leverage translate into real amounts?
  3. 3. Have I already tried the platform on a demo account?
  4. 4. Which platform will I use (MT4, MT5, WebTrader, app) and does the account support it?
  5. 5. Have I read the terms, checked which entity would provide the service, and checked availability in my country?

Other accounts

HFM also offers other accounts, such as InfinityX or Shield 500, with specific terms. Read those terms on hfm.com before any decision.
